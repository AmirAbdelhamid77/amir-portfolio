# 21 — Authored style recipes / art direction experiments

Recipes below are *starting seeds*, not a global theme. No exact color/typography token should replace inspected Hansa tokens.

## 01. Botanical Digitalism
Organic filament branches meet sharp technical nodes and restrained desaturated materials. Key directional lighting; near-field tactile detail; procedural growth followed by a quick settle. Best: concept networks, dependencies, learning progression. Risk: falsely implying hierarchy; render typed edges accurately.

## 02. Architectural Data Museum
Volumetric rooms, matte surfaces, museum plaques in DOM, soft directional light, camera rail with skip. Best: guided Fabric architecture or portfolio case studies. Risk: overlong navigation; provide index and instant jump.

## 03. Precision Instrument Lab
Exploded machined parts, orthographic/perspective hybrid, fine outlines, bright informative cuts. Best: SQL execution, DAX context, Delta lifecycle. Risk: mechanics imply too literal a physical process; mark analogy.

## 04. Topographic Intelligence
Spatial contours, translucent sections, cross-cut overlays, clear legend. Best: real geography or multi-scale structures with justified topology; use care with charts.

## 05. Light Sculpture Editorial
Typography and limited geometry form a single strong silhouette, asymmetrical negative space, dramatic but readable contrast. Best: landing, case study intros, daily insight. Risk: text clipped by camera or poor mobile legibility.

## 06. Glass Observatory
Frosted framing elements, sparse luminous data relationships, strict contrast management. Best: graph discovery with contextual surfaces. Risk: busy bloom and blur overwhelm label readability.

## 07. Mechanical Time Engine
Concentric temporal layers, user-controlled scrub, precise date labels, snap to keyframes. Best: timelined experiments, historic versions, process progression. Risk: conflating chronological and hierarchical relations.

## 08. Paper & Clay Knowledge Theatre
Soft tactile depth, subtle shadows, cut-paper reveals and calm transitions. Best: educational explainers needing approachability, reduced GPU use. Risk: becoming flat card stacks; use authored shapes and asymmetric stage.

## 09. Computational Filaments
Actual typed relationships form glowing traceable fibers around matte anchor objects. Best: lineage, data movement and query execution. Risk: unverified 'packet animations' implying real-time throughput.

## 10. Temporal Prism
Transparent layered scenes with explicit time-slice controls, consistent axes and annotated states. Best: Delta history, version diffs, project timelines. Risk: glass materials expensive and confusing with too many layers.

## 11. Editorial Split-World
Spatial visualization on one side, precise text/chart on the other, synchronized states and mobile sequencing. Best: highly technical teaching with both emotional and exact modes. Risk: two stories competing; hierarchy must be clear.

## 12. Living Typographic Network
Words are 3D-ish anchor sculptures, relations thread between exact labels; interactions rearrange typography itself. Best: curated topic discovery, homepage micro-experience. Risk: accessibility; DOM text remains primary.

## Recipe selection protocol
Choose based on content topology, task, surrounding site repetition ledger and device cost. Build a light and dark candidate only where Hansa supports both. Reject default neon/purple/glass on every surface. A rich scene can be calm, bright, minimal in palette and still spatially sophisticated.
