Operating System

Data + Narrative + Visuals → Understanding → Decision → Action

Question → Audience → Big Idea → Minimum Evidence → Metrics → Message-to-Visual → Format → Delivery → Rigor

Do not start from a slide or a chart. Start from the decision and the person who owns it.

Canonical Map

Four sections. Twelve parts. No duplicate blurbs.

Section 1 · Strategy

What the story is, who it is for, which evidence and metrics survive.

  • Part I — Foundations — Big Idea, three-minute story, storytelling ≠ spin
  • Part II — Know Your Audience — personas, ADEPT, Self / Friend / Skeptic
  • Part III — Building the Narrative — problem → driver → impact → action; no data dump
  • Part IV — Metrics & Statistics — absolute vs relative, mean vs median, practical vs statistical significance

Section 2 · Craft

Turn surviving evidence into visuals and a channel.

  • Part V — Visualization — visualize the message; McCandless; Insight → Impact → Action
  • Part VI — Choosing the Communication Format — oral vs written vs notebook; combine formats

Section 3 · Delivery

Write it, speak it, keep it rerunnable.

  • Part VII — Written Reports — informational / analytical / final / summary; 1–3–25
  • Part VIII — Reproducibility & Rigor — never make the dataset the only record
  • Part IX — Oral Presentations in Practice — one message per slide; insight headlines
