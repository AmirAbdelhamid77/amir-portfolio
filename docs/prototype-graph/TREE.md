## What this map is

The learner route is `/prototype/network/`. It draws **containment only**: parent → child edges from the corpus registries. Citations, prerequisites, co-citation, TF-IDF candidates and Atlas review relations are not structure and are not drawn, except for declared “see also” lines on the selected node.

Two navigational layers are synthetic and marked in the payload: URL-derived source groups and repeated-chapter-title groups. Programme lenses are projections of declared programme subjects, not new containment claims.

- No DP-700 programme is invented; the corpus does not declare one.
- Academy courseware is shown only in the Academy programme lens; the subject lens stays subject-declared.
- The 9 empty packs are documented above, not shown as dead ends.
- The 25 Notion source pages are not area-grouped because their URLs contain no documentation area.
- The graph is not an exam blueprint and does not claim every Fabric pack is DP-600-specific.

## Learner view contract

The map is an explorer, not a completeness diagram. These rules are enforced by `lib/knowledge-graph/tree-model.ts`, `tree-layout.ts`, `tree-labels.ts` and `tests/knowledge/tree-map.test.mjs`.

- Default subject lens: the 8 subject roots only. Every root is labelled, labels do not overlap, and the URL carries no focus.
- Opening a branch is an accordion by default; "Keep branches open" disables it. Each branch pages 12 children at a time with a "+N more" control, and the whole view stops at 150 nodes.
- Ring = depth, angle = the parent's wedge share, node size = √(visible descendants), colour = root subject. Ring radii follow the deepest open level so shallow views use the canvas.
- A short deterministic `d3-force` pass (collide, weak link, weak radial) settles positions. The same open set always produces the same layout.
- Labels are drawn for the focus, its children and the path to the root. They truncate at 24 characters, show the full title on hover, and are skipped rather than overlapped. Root labels stay under zoom until a branch is focused.
- On a narrow map (tablet, or the side card open) the label layer is counter-scaled up to 1.5× so text stays near 11–12 px on screen; the collision pass uses the same scale. Below 768 px labels are hidden by design and the List tab carries the names.
- The map is a single tab stop: arrow keys move between siblings and into children, Enter/Space activates, and Escape closes the details card and returns focus to its node or list row. The List keeps a conventional tab order.
- Only containment edges are drawn. Declared "see also" lines appear dashed for the selected node; derived, candidate and Atlas-review edges never load on this route.
- Lenses are "By subject" (default) and "By programme" (DP-600, Fabric RTI, Corpus Academy), one at a time.
- `?focus=<id>&open=<id,id>` restores the view. "Show everything" is an explicit, labels-off overview outside the 150-node cap and is never the default.
- Four shapes only: root (double ring), container (ring), unit (dot), source/page (square). "+N more" is a control, not a node.
- At 390 px the List tab is the default; the Map tab stays as a labels-off spatial overview, with names in the card and the List.
