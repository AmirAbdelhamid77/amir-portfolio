## Open caveats

- The polish rules are dead bytes in the public stylesheet: 5,041 B of 253,060 B,
  30 rules. Splitting them into a second stylesheet was rejected for the same
  reason as R5 - the stage ships one stylesheet and a second request costs more
  than the dead rules. This trade is measured, not hidden.
- The clock tick is deliberately 10 px, one step below the label role, because
  `THU 05 NOV` wraps inside its 76 px cell at 11 px. That is the only place the
  nine-role scale is not literally nine values.
- One control legend (`What does the second team need?`) stays 11 px uppercase
  as part of the instrument's label system. It is named here so the "no prose
  below 13 px" claim is not read as "nothing below 13 px".
- The decision-model colon is CSS `content`, so it is not in the accessible text
  and does not change a copy review; the `<dl>` structure carries the pairing.
- The two accessibility corrections ship without a flag. They cannot be toggled
  off; reverting them is a code change. Both are string-level and were reviewed
  as such.
- Sighted readers still see the reading's title twice (peek and reading); the
  `sr-only` prefix only disambiguates the outline. Removing the visible duplicate
  would be a composition change, and R6 does not make composition changes.
- The archive route is untouched by the layer (it is not `.hansa-stage`) and was
  not re-captured; R6's surface is the three-scene stage.
- Browser/AT not exercised: no screen-reader run, no Safari/Firefox, no touch
  hardware, no forced-colors pass. Existing forced-colors rules were kept, not
  extended.
- Performance is one local run per build, not field data. The public LCP spread
  (776 / 792 / 1,152 ms) is local noise around the R5 756 ms; the polish build's
  716 ms is not evidence of a speed-up, because it serves the same bytes.
- `verify-knowledge-atlas.mjs` is blocked by five untracked `EGM.md` documents
  created at 14:12 today (including `app/EGM.md`). They are not part of R6 and
  were left in place for their owner.

  promotion question above. No flag becomes the public default and nothing is
