## What the gap means for practice design

1. **No blueprint-weighted mock is offered.** Reproducing 25–30% semantic models from 13
   questions would require either repetition that pretends to be a sample, or invented
   questions. Both would misrepresent the bank.
2. **Timed sessions are labelled "custom practice simulation"** in the UI, the session
   label, and the results screen. No percentage is described as a predicted exam score.
3. **Semantic-model study is flagged as thin** on the hub, the mock set-up and here, so a
   reader does not infer coverage from a large total.
4. **DP-700-adjacent material is kept and labelled.** 90 questions declare
   DP-700 as primary; they are excluded from "DP-600 focused" sessions but remain available
   in Fabric Extended scope, because the engineering depth is genuinely useful — it is just
   not what DP-600 measures.
