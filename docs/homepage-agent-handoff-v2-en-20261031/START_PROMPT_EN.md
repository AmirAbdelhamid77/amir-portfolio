The intended experience is a full-viewport beUI Timeline, a daily Card Dealer opened intentionally from the date, a scroll transition into an Intelligent Interactive Knowledge Graph as the primary website entry point, and a concise closing. Preserve the detailed UI, motion, focus, mobile, theme, state-restoration and future-extension requirements.

**Before constructing the Atlas, recursively analyze the entire eligible website corpus.** Inventory and search metadata alone are insufficient. Produce a unit-level analysis ledger, canonical concepts, resource mappings, evidence-backed typed relationship candidates, ambiguity/contradiction reports and coverage evidence. Do not stop at a category catalogue. The graph must support explainable connections, contextual next steps, goal routes, cross-domain bridges and deliberate progressive exploration.

Preserve publication, scientific, rights and assessment gates. Complete graph preparation and an isolated local editorial preview even when public relationship publication awaits review. Never fabricate relationships or claim that candidate extraction is human approval.

**Initial assignment: visual-review mode.** Deliver the full-corpus analysis artifacts and a five-frame storyboard plus a standalone interactive prototype using a clearly identified real subset. The prototype must demonstrate date → cards → graph context → a supported learning action → return, plus evidence inspection and a coverage-aware goal/next-step interaction. Distinguish unreviewed editorial data from the public projection. Prepare the extension contracts, particularly reuse of KnowledgeDealer from additional contexts. Test keyboard, mobile, reduced motion and interrupted transitions. Report the full-analysis status separately from the prototype's visual/data coverage. Do not integrate the prototype into the live application or deploy it in this mode.

If the owner assigns **full local implementation**, carry the same specifications through all required implementation phases, integrate all eligible content and available published graph data, complete the acceptance matrix and deliver a local preview and evidence. A sample prototype is then an intermediate milestone. Deployment remains separately authorized.
